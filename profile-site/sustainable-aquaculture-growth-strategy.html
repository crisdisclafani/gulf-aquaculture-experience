Sustainability in practice

Built for
Tomorrow

At Gulf Aquaculture, sustainability isn’t an add-on — it’s built into the way we think, work, and grow. From the design of our hatcheries to the resilience of our broodstock, every decision is made with tomorrow in mind.

We don’t just raise fish. We raise the bar for what aquaculture can be — for people, planet, and prosperity.

Future Growth

Our vision has always been clear:
validate first, then grow with integrity.

With Phase II now approved, we’re preparing for the next chapter - one that remains grounded in our core principles.

Vertical
Expansion

We’re building more of the value chain - including transport, genetics, and off-take partnerships - to increase resilience and deliver long-term value.

New
Sites

Future facilities will be located nationally and regionally, selected based on environmental suitability and alignment with our low-impact model.

Broodstock
Development

We’re advancing broodstock lines to improve climate resilience and enable year-round spawning, supporting stable, scalable supply.

Strategic
Partnerships

Through public-private collaboration, we aim to contribute to national food security, biodiversity, and the long-term future of Gulf aquaculture.

We welcome conversations that move aquaculture forward.

Whether you're an investor who shares our long-term vision, a researcher exploring native species, or a policymaker shaping the future of food — we believe progress happens through partnership.

We’re building more than a business. We’re building trust, resilience, and a model that others can build on. If that resonates with you, we’d love to hear from you.

Our regional footprint: UAE, Oman, Malaysia and Indonesia

  • Oman50 tonnes/month of Seabream. Red Snapper and Seabream fry supply agreement from Dec 2025.
  • Balghailam Island (UAE)Grouper and Red Snapper hatchery and nursery site. Co-invested with Hibiscus Capital Investment and operated by GA.
  • Abu Dhabi HQ (UAE)Head office for Gulf Aquaculture. Oversight of Grouper and Red Snapper programs.
  • SKMRC (UAE)Government-owned hatchery operated by GA on a 15-year concession.
  • Juvenile Supply (UAE)Supplying Grouper and Red Snapper juveniles to a local company since 2024. Projected at 7 million/year by 2026.
  • MalaysiaRed Snapper hatchery co-invested with Aquagrow International. Producing 1 million fry/month since 2023.
  • IndonesiaGrouper hatchery with Aquagrow International. Producing 300,000 fry/month. Launching 2026.
