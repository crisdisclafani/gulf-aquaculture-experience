Hamra Malabar Red Snapper

Lutjanus malabaricus

In production

Enquire about Hamra fry →
Species in motion

Hamra, from hatchery to sea

Scientific & common names

  • Hamra — local name
  • Malabar Red Snapper — common name
  • Lutjanus malabaricus — scientific name

Biological characteristics

A hardy warm-water species with favourable growth characteristics and attractive culinary traits.

Natural habitat & distribution

Found across the Indo-West Pacific, including the Arabian Gulf, on coastal and offshore reef grounds. See it on the distribution map

Environmental tolerances

To be supplied by Gulf Aquaculture

Commercial farming characteristics

Suited to low-density cage systems and well-managed land-based systems, with efficient feed conversion and predictable harvest values. Strong commercial acceptance in Middle Eastern markets.

Production methodology

Fry are reared with live feed systems and microbiology monitoring during the larval phase, supporting stable development through the most sensitive early growth stages.

Fry availability & specifications

  • Grouper and Red Snapper hatchery and nursery at Balghailam Island
  • Red Snapper juveniles supplied to a local company since 2024 — projected 7 million/year by 2026 (with Grouper)
  • Malaysia hatchery with Aquagrow International: 1 million fry/month since 2023
  • Oman: Red Snapper and Seabream fry supply agreement from Dec 2025
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Hamra?

Hamra is the local name for Malabar Red Snapper (Lutjanus malabaricus). A hardy and fast-growing species ideal for sea cage culture.

Does Gulf Aquaculture supply Hamra fry?

Yes — Hamra is part of our native species programme in Abu Dhabi. Send an enquiry with quantity, size and timing for availability and specifications.

How is Hamra farmed?

Suited to low-density cage systems and well-managed land-based systems, with efficient feed conversion and predictable harvest values. Strong commercial acceptance in Middle Eastern markets.

Can you deliver fry outside the UAE?

We supply regional clients across the GCC and beyond (for example our fry supply agreement in Oman). Tell us your location in the enquiry and we will confirm logistics.
