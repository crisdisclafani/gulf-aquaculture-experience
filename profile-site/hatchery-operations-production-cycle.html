Our operations

We designed our facilities to do one thing well: raise healthy fish in sustainable, replicable systems.

  1. Hatchery & Nursery:

    Controlled environments for larvae and fry.

  2. Live Feed Systems:

    Rotifers, copepods, algae production.

  3. Biosecurity:

    Closed-loop systems, advanced filtration, and strict protocols.

  4. Laboratories:

    Water quality and microbiology testing.

  5. Traceability:

    SFS Trace system ensures full lifecycle transparency.

The production cycle: from broodstock to distribution

Each stage of our operation reflects a clear purpose and a shared responsibility to the Gulf. In the hatchery, our teams begin early — checking water parameters, preparing live feed cultures, and monitoring larval development. This work is methodical and essential, creating the foundation for healthy fish and healthy ecosystems.

As fry grow, they are transitioned to nursery tanks and eventually to sea cages, where they continue to mature in conditions that mimic their natural habitat. This low-impact approach is not only efficient — it respects the Gulf’s unique marine environment by working in harmony with it, rather than forcing artificial outcomes.

Behind the scenes, our laboratory teams track microbial health, monitor water quality, and ensure every fish can be traced from broodstock to harvest. Our systems reduce energy usage, recycle water, and eliminate the need for unnecessary antibiotics. Every step is intentional, designed to reduce stress on fish — and on the environment.

Infrastructure & technology

The systems behind healthy fish

Compact, biosecure and replicable: the same technical backbone runs across our hatcheries and is designed to be repeated at future sites.

Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS)

Integrated RAS and flow-through systems keep temperature, salinity and oxygen stable for every life stage, while recirculating and treating water to cut water and energy use.

Balghailam · SKMRC

Water intake & filtration

Seawater is drawn in, mechanically filtered and treated through biofilters and UV sterilisation before it reaches broodstock, larvae or nursery tanks.

Balghailam · SKMRC

Water-quality monitoring

On-site laboratories track key parameters — such as temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH, salinity and nitrogen compounds — so every tank is checked, logged and adjusted daily.

14 labs at SKMRC

Biosecurity & fish health

Separated production zones, controlled access and early pathogen screening by our microbiology lab protect stock without unnecessary antibiotics.

All sites

Live-feed production

Dedicated algae and live-feed culture — grown alongside the larval rearing halls — provides the right nutrition at the right size for each larval stage.

Balghailam · SKMRC

Environmental management

Native species, low-impact sea cages with natural water exchange, treated discharge and energy-conscious systems keep our footprint on the Gulf as light as possible.
