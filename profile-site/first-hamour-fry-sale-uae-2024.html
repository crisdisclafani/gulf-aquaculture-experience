First commercial sale of Hamour fry to the local market

Our Balghailam Island hatchery proved the concept: native Hamour fry, produced in Abu Dhabi, sold to UAE farms.

In 2024 Gulf Aquaculture completed its first sale of Hamour (orange-spotted grouper) fry to the local market.

The milestone was the proof of concept for our Balghailam Island hatchery, a 6,000 m² facility with integrated RAS and flow-through systems.

Since then we have been supplying Grouper and Red Snapper juveniles to a local company and scaling up production of larvae and fry for commercial farms in the Gulf.
