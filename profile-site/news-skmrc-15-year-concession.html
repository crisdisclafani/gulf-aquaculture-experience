Gulf Aquaculture takes over the Sheikh Khalifa Marine Research Centre

A 15-year concession gives Gulf Aquaculture exclusive management of the largest hatchery and nursery facility in the UAE and the wider Gulf.

In November 2025 the UAE Government awarded Gulf Aquaculture a long-term lease for the Sheikh Khalifa Marine Research Centre (SKMRC) in Umm Al Quwain, under a 15-year concession.

SKMRC offers approximately 18,000 m² of hatchery space in two production streams, 14 laboratories provisioned for ISO/IEC 17025 and the Marine Innovation Park.

Our teams manage broodstocks of Seabream, Hamour, Shaari, Mangrove Snapper, Mullet, Cobia and Naisrah, with nursery operations focused on Hamour, Red Snapper and Shaari and a target of 20 million fry in 2026.
