From broodstock to distribution

Every stage reflects a clear purpose — and a shared responsibility to the Gulf.

Select a phase around the cage. Throughout, our laboratory teams track microbial health, monitor water quality, and ensure every fish can be traced from broodstock to harvest.

Each stage of our operation reflects a clear purpose and a shared responsibility to the Gulf. In the hatchery, our teams begin early — checking water parameters, preparing live feed cultures, and monitoring larval development. This work is methodical and essential, creating the foundation for healthy fish and healthy ecosystems.

As fry grow, they are transitioned to nursery tanks and eventually to sea cages, where they continue to mature in conditions that mimic their natural habitat. This low-impact approach is not only efficient — it respects the Gulf's unique marine environment by working in harmony with it, rather than forcing artificial outcomes.

Behind the scenes, our laboratory teams track microbial health, monitor water quality, and ensure every fish can be traced from broodstock to harvest. Our systems reduce energy usage, recycle water, and eliminate the need for unnecessary antibiotics. Every step is intentional, designed to reduce stress on fish — and on the environment.

Our systems are modular and repeatable — ready to be replicated across future sites.
