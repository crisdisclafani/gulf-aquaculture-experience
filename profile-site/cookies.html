How this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

Draft for the concept website — to be reviewed by Gulf Aquaculture’s legal advisor

What we use

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

It stores two small preferences in your browser’s local storage, only on your device: the visitor profile you choose under “Access by profile” (to highlight relevant pages) and nothing else that identifies you. You can clear it at any time with “Clear ×” or by clicking Home.

Embedded videos

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and are only loaded when you press play. Once you play a video, YouTube (Google) may set cookies under its own policy.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address to deliver them.

Managing preferences

You can block or delete cookies and local storage from your browser settings. If analytics are added in the future (as foreseen in the website brief), this policy and a consent banner will be updated first.
