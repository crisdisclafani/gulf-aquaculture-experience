Gulf Aquaculture receives BAP certification for both its assets

Balghailam Island and the Sheikh Khalifa Marine Research Centre are now certified under Best Aquaculture Practices.

In March 2026 Gulf Aquaculture received Best Aquaculture Practices (BAP) certification for its two assets: the Balghailam Island hatchery and the Sheikh Khalifa Marine Research Centre (SKMRC).

BAP is one of the most widely recognised third-party certification programmes for aquaculture, covering environmental responsibility, animal welfare, food safety and traceability.

For our clients the certification confirms what our operating model was built for: biosecure, traceable production of native Gulf species, monitored by our own water-quality and microbiology laboratories at every stage.
