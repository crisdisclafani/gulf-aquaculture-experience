03 · Assets under management

Two assets.
One model.

Balghailam Island and the Sheikh Khalifa Marine Research Centre — compact, biosecure and replicable.

Systems that support people.

Balghailam Island

A model for sustainable marine hatcheries in the region.

Instead of scaling vertically or chasing complexity, we've built a system aligned with nature — focused on fish health, biosecurity, and operational reliability. Compact, efficient, and replicable.

Key stats

  • 6,000 m² of hatchery space
  • Dedicated rooms for live feed and algae production
  • Integrated RAS & flow-through systems
  • Automated filtration, UV sterilisation, and real-time water quality sensors
  • Biosecure broodstock housing and spawning zones
  • Modular nursery capacity for phased growth
  • Fully equipped lab for water quality and microbiology

Hatchery infrastructure

Broodstock Management

Four 20 m³ tanks hold genetically diverse broodstock, conditioned for Gulf waters. Climate-controlled rooms support consistent spawning, including off-season cycles.

Larval Rearing Systems

Eight 10 m³ tanks provide stable environments for early-stage development — ensuring strong survival, growth, and low-stress rearing.

Nursery Operations

Twenty-four 10 m³ tanks prepare juveniles for sea cages, with minimal handling and high health standards maintained throughout.

Sustainable Feed Systems

Three 2 m³ tanks produce rotifers and copepods. Eight outdoor tanks grow Nannochloropsis sp., forming the foundation of our live feed system.

Water Quality & Microbiology Labs

Our on-site lab monitors water quality and fish health. Two functional areas track key parameters and identify pathogens early.

  • CFU counts
  • Microbial cultivation
  • Wet mounts
  • Antibiograms for treatment guidance

Systems That Support People

Dry Lab & Traceability Systems — supports planning and analytics, integrated with SFS Trace to track fish from broodstock to harvest.

Staff Facilities — dedicated spaces for welfare, training, and teamwork.

Sheikh Khalifa Marine Research Centre

The pinnacle of Middle Eastern hatchery technology and design.

GA has been operating the Sheikh Khalifa Marine Research Centre [SKMRC] since November 2025.

Innovation at its heart

  • Fourteen dedicated laboratories provisioned for compliance with ISO/IEC 17025
  • 1,200 m² of office space
  • Fully furnished staff canteen and academic spaces
  • The Marine Innovation Park — 14 laboratories and a science discovery division for research and younger audiences

Hatchery infrastructure · ≈18,000 m² in two production streams

Broodstock Management

The team manages and breeds Seabream, Hamour, Shaari, Mangrove Snapper, Mullet, Cobia, and Naisrah.

Larval Rearing Systems

  • Production of 3 species throughout the year
  • Annual production capacity of 20+ million larvae

Nursery Operations

  • Currently focusing on Hamour, Red Snapper and Shari
  • Target production of 20 million fry in 2026

Sustainable Feed Systems

  • Live feed: rotifers, Artemia, copepods
  • 7 species of live algae, year-round

Laboratories & Diagnostics

Microbial testing capabilities include:

  • Viruses
  • Bacteria
  • Parasites
  • Water quality parameters

Phase 01 · Phase 02

Each production stream combines broodstock, hatchery, nursery and weaning areas with office, plant and generator rooms.
