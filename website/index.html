    We're doing things differently

    We believe success starts small.

    1. Prioritise native speciesLike grouper and red snapper.
    2. Keep upland infrastructure minimalJust hatchery and nursery.
    3. Move juveniles to sea cages earlyReducing energy use.
    4. Grow graduallyThrough proof of concept, validation and iteration.

    We call this: Local Species in Local Waters.

    A region hungry for change

    Demand is growing. Wild stocks are not.

    Seafood demand in the UAE grows every year, while local fisheries decline. We saw this not as a failure — but as an opportunity to rethink, simplify and rebuild from first principles.

    8%annual growth in UAE seafood demand
    900ktonnes expected by 2030
    50%less wild Gulf fish stocks since 1990

    How this demo works

    Look & feel

    • Based on the GA Company Profile: white and cream pages, deep navy, orange–amber accents, teal diamonds, serif headlines and handwritten highlights.
    • Photos marked "Client photo" come from the Company Profile; everything else is a placeholder.

    • The three buttons under the menu highlight that profile's pages in the menu, sub-menus and page sections, and open a personal pathway bar. The choice is remembered across pages.

    Interactive pieces

    • Phases circle: click a segment (or use ← →); auto tour can be paused.
    • 3D experience: the "Egg to Harvest" WebGL journey, embedded; the station buttons jump straight to each scene.
    • Enquiry form: tick one or more topics — each is routed to its team and shows the relevant fields.

    Placeholders & CMS

    • Dashed boxes with ▶ = video / animation / map to be produced. Figures come from the Company Profile; items in [brackets] are to be supplied.
    • Arabic labels are a draft for native review.
    • Each page declares its CMS template (T1–T9); data lives in assets/js/data.js.
