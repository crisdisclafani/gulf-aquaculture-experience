    How this demo works

    Audience colour system

    ColourAudienceWhat they look for
    Lagoon TealAquaculture farms & operatorsProduction capacity, species availability, fry quality, biosecurity, technical capabilities, commercial enquiries
    Desert GoldGovernment authorities & investorsFood security, regional capacity, infrastructure, sustainability, strategic importance
    Coral ReefUniversities & research institutionsScientific expertise, laboratories, research programmes, collaboration

    Navigation

    • Each menu item shows small coloured bars = the audiences it serves.
    • Inside every mega-menu, coloured dots mark each page's audience.
    • Choosing a profile ("I am —") highlights the relevant pages everywhere, dims the rest and opens a personal pathway bar.
    • Every enquiry type is coloured by audience and shows its own form fields.

    Placeholders

    • Dashed boxes with ▶ label = video, animation, interactive map or diagram (Part 4 workstream).
    • Figures, dates, addresses and headlines are placeholders, not real data.
    • Arabic labels (عربي) are a draft to show RTL layout — they need native review.

    CMS roadmap

    • One HTML file per page; shared styles and scripts live in /assets, site data in assets/js/data.js.
    • Every editable block is tagged data-cms; every section declares its template (T1–T9 from the brief).
    • Menu, audience pathways and enquiry categories are data-driven → editable menus and taxonomies in the backend.
    • New species, facilities and research programmes = new entries of an existing template, no developer needed.
