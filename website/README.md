# Gulf Aquaculture — Website concept demo (static HTML)

Open `index.html` in a browser. No server needed.

## Pages
| File | Section | CMS template |
|---|---|---|
| index.html | Home | T1 Homepage |
| about.html | About Us | T2 Corporate Overview |
| operations.html | Operations & Facilities | T3 Facility Overview |
| species.html | Species (index) | T5 Species Profile |
| species-*.html (×4) | Single species profile | T5 Species Profile |
| science.html | Science & Technology | T4 Technical Capabilities |
| research.html | Research & Innovation | T6 Research & Innovation |
| sustainability.html | Sustainability & Food Security | T7 Sustainability |
| news.html / news-article.html | News list / article | T8 News & Insights |
| contact.html | Contact & Enquiries | T9 Contact & Enquiries |

## Structure
- `assets/css/style.css` — every style, shared by all pages
- `assets/js/data.js` — site data: menu, audiences, species, enquiry types, news (in the CMS → menus, taxonomies, content entries)
- `assets/js/main.js` — shared behaviour (menu, audience pathways, forms, filters)

## Audience colour system
- Teal — Aquaculture farms & operators
- Gold — Government authorities & investors
- Coral — Universities & research institutions

The choice made in "I am —" is remembered across pages (localStorage): menu, sub-menu and page sections show what is relevant.

## Placeholders
Dashed boxes with ▶ = video / animation / interactive map / diagram. Figures, dates, names and addresses in brackets are placeholders. The Arabic labels are a draft (native review required).

## Notes for the CMS
- Editable blocks are marked `data-cms="…"`; every page declares its template in `<body data-template>`.
- New species, facilities or research programmes = new entries of an existing template.
- In the CMS, header and footer are rendered server-side (better SEO); here they are generated by `main.js` from `data.js`.
